Download Safeguards

Protecting downloadable documents.

Downloaded files without safeguards can leave company systems without the company’s knowledge.

What you can do

At least one is recommended for deal-processing roles (e.g. underwriters, sales):

  • Change document access to view-only.
  • If documents must remain downloadable, two practical options:
    1. Browser and device controls that restrict personnel from moving company data outside approved systems.
    2. Watermark files with the company name or logo.
  • Other: We’re happy to review alternatives or schedule a brief call to discuss practical options. Contact us.
How you can do it

Path 1: Browser and Device Controls

Safeguards must restrict personnel from moving company data outside approved systems. At a minimum, controls must address:

  • Web & Domain Restrictions: Block access to personal email, personal cloud storage, and unauthorized file-transfer services.
  • USB & Removable Storage Restrictions: Block or restrict copying company files to physical media.

Options to look into, depending on your current setup:

  • Endpoint/MDM Policies: Microsoft Intune, Jamf, Hexnode, or ManageEngine. Centrally manage company devices; can cover both web and USB restrictions.
  • Windows Group Policy (GPO): Built into Windows; can block USB storage and restrict browser settings on company computers.
  • Enterprise Browsers: Island or Talon. Control what users can access, upload, and download within the browser.
  • Web/DNS Filtering: Palo Alto Prisma, Cloudflare One, or DNSFilter. Block personal email, cloud storage, and file-transfer sites at the network or device level.
  • Conditional Access: Okta, JumpCloud, or Entra ID. Restrict logins to company systems to approved, managed devices only.
  • Virtual Desktops (VDI): Windows 365, Azure Virtual Desktop, or Amazon WorkSpaces. Keep company files inside a controlled workspace rather than on the local machine.

Comparable solutions that achieve the same security outcomes may also be used.

AND/OR

Path 2: Watermarking

Stamp files with your company logo before they reach the system of record, or at the time of download. A visible mark shows where the document came from and discourages anyone from forwarding or reusing it elsewhere. Examples include Aquamark or any equivalent watermarking tool.
