Acceptable Safeguards

Downloading is allowed. If non-management staff can download application files, choose one safeguard so an unprotected copy can’t leave the company from their computer. Owners and management can keep download access.

You only need to choose one:

View-only accessSystem of record

Transition to view-only and disable file downloads for non-management roles. No watermarking or browser controls are needed.

Evidence we accept: a screenshot of a record, taken while logged in as the record owner, showing that downloading is not available.

WatermarkingSystem of record

Keep files downloadable and apply watermarks before files enter your system of record, or when they’re downloaded. Watermarking is accepted as a reasonable deterrent against unauthorized sharing. Watermarks should include your company name or logo. Aquamark or any equivalent service is acceptable. No browser or device controls are needed.

Evidence we accept: a watermarked sample file plus a screenshot of the workflow or integration that applies the watermark automatically. Watermarking a file by hand doesn’t count, because someone handles the unprotected original first.

Browser and device controlsEmail inboxSystem of record

Keep files downloadable, and stop them from leaving the computer. This prevents staff from sending files to themselves or outside the company. No watermarking is needed.

  • Browser: block personal email and personal cloud storage websites.
  • Device: block USB file transfers.

Evidence we accept: screenshots showing both controls are in place to restrict staff from transferring files to personal accounts or external storage.

Tools that can help

  • Endpoint/MDM policies: Microsoft Intune, Jamf, Hexnode, or ManageEngine
  • Windows Group Policy (GPO): built into Windows
  • Enterprise browsers: Island or Talon
  • Web/DNS filtering: Palo Alto Prisma, Cloudflare One, or DNSFilter
Other safeguards

Other safeguards are acceptable if they meet the same goal. Send us a message to discuss your approach.

If you receive submissions by email:

Browser and device controls (above) also cover your inbox.

Automate email intakeEmail inbox

You can keep receiving submissions by email, as long as no one works from the intake mailbox. Set up automation to route files directly into your system of record. Your inbox then needs no watermarking or browser controls, but files still need safeguards once they’re in your system of record.

Evidence we accept: a screenshot of the routing setup (or routing logs showing submissions moving automatically into your system of record), and of who can access the intake mailbox.

Tools that can help

  • Inbound email parsing: Postmark, SendGrid, Heron, Email Gateway
  • Automation tools: Zapier, Make, n8n, Microsoft Power Automate
  • Built-in email intake in your CRM or loan platform
Watermark before the inboxEmail inbox

If people open submissions in the inbox, watermark files before they reach it. This also covers files later stored in your system of record, so no other safeguard is needed. Watermarks should include your company name or logo. Aquamark or any equivalent service is acceptable.

Evidence we accept: a watermarked sample file plus a screenshot of the workflow or integration that applies the watermark before files reach the inbox. Watermarking a file by hand doesn’t count, because someone handles the unprotected original first.
