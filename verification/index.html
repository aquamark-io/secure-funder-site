How Verification Works

A brief, structured walkthrough conducted via screen share.

Live Screen Share

Via Google Meet

15–30 Minutes

Quick and focused

Not Recorded

All information is confidential

Our Approach

Any member of your organization with appropriate system access may conduct the session
Your representative shares their screen — we do not access your systems directly
Only information necessary to verify each control is displayed
Sessions may be paused or rescheduled at any time, including between individual requirements
The objective is to work with your organization to achieve certification
Reference
Sample Verification Procedures

Representative examples of what is reviewed during a session. The complete list of verifiable controls is available on the Requirements page.

Multi-Factor Authentication

Verifying MFA is enabled for email and CRM access.

What We Ask

Log out of your email and CRM, then log back in while sharing your screen. We confirm that a multi-factor prompt is triggered during the login process.

Email Submission Handling

Verifying how broker submissions via email are processed.

If Automated Processing

A test submission is sent in real time to confirm it is routed to your CRM without human intervention.

If No Email Submissions

We review your ISO guidelines or broker documentation confirming that portal and/or API are the only accepted submission methods.

If Using Email Watermarking

Verified in real time by sending a test submission and confirming attachments are watermarked upon arrival.

Role-Based Access Controls

Verifying that sensitive information is only visible to those with business necessity.

What We Ask

Using admin access, log in as users in various roles to demonstrate that sensitive fields (SSN, DOB, etc.) are restricted to personnel with a documented business need.

For documents: Show that files are either view-only, or if downloadable, they're already watermarked or get watermarked at time of download.

User Verification

Confirming that system access is limited to appropriate personnel.

What We Ask

A review of your user list to confirm no external entities have access — particularly brokerages, software platforms, or other lending companies — including those under common ownership.

If related entities have access: The business rationale is reviewed to determine whether it meets the intent of the requirement.
