New Partner Due Diligence: A Baseline Checklist

Nine checks before onboarding a broker, funder, or other partner.

Christina Duncan
Program Director, Secure Funder™

Reputation and referrals go a long way in alternative finance, but neither is a substitute for basic due diligence and judgement. Below is a baseline — not an exhaustive due diligence standard — for vetting a new partner before onboarding.

The Baseline

  • Business registration. Confirm the company has an active filing with the appropriate Secretary of State.
  • Ownership. Identify the company’s owners or principals and confirm the information is consistent with available records.
  • Industry references. One or two contacts familiar with the company or its principals.
  • Live verification. A short video call to verify identity.
  • Online presence. Company website, business email domain, social media, directory listings.
  • Online search. Look for complaints, enforcement actions, lawsuits, or other relevant public information.
  • Credentials, where applicable. Verify applicable licenses, registrations, or certifications.
  • Data security. Ask how sensitive business and merchant information is handled.
  • Documentation. Review partner agreements before signing.

My Final Thoughts

No checklist replaces judgment, and none of this catches everything. But a consistent baseline gives every new relationship the same starting point.
