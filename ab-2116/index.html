California AB 2116: Readiness Checklist

An industry resource.

Christina Duncan
Program Director, Secure Funder™

Brokers and providers in MCA, factoring, ABL or equipment financing will need a CFL license soon.

Key dates

Late 2027The latest you should file your CFL application through NMLS. Plan for a wait of several months.
Jan 1, 2028Funding agreements with unlicensed providers become unenforceable, unless an application is already on file.
Jul 1, 2028CFL license officially required.
Mar 15, 2029First annual activity report due to the DFPI.

Everyone

Funders

Brokers & ISOs

Still to come

DFPI rulemaking in 2027, including public comment, will set:

  • Application forms and fees
  • APR display rules for broker websites
  • What counts as a complete application before January 1, 2028

For general information only, not legal advice. Talk to qualified counsel about how AB 2116 applies to your business.
