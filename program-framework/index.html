Download Safeguards

Full details

If documents must remain downloadable, at least one of the following is in place.

  1. 1

    Document watermarking

    Files are watermarked with the company name or logo before reaching the system of record, or at the time of download.

  2. 2

    Browser and device controls

    Browser restrictions block personal email and cloud storage domains, and device controls prevent file transfers through USB.

  3. 3

    Other

    Another safeguard or approach that meets the intent of the requirement, subject to review.
