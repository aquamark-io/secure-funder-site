Program Framework

Submission

An application package arrives: the application, bank statements, and supporting documents. It comes in by email, directly into the CRM or LOS, or both.

Email inbox

The package arrives as attachments in an email inbox. While in the inbox, files can be downloaded, forwarded, or saved outside company systems.

Manual handling

Staff open the email and download the attachments to a local device before uploading them to the CRM or LOS.

Automated routing

Rules or integrations (e.g. Salesforce, Zoho) move attachments from the inbox into the CRM or LOS without anyone downloading them.

Safeguards needed

Files pass through a local device, so a download safeguard must be in place.

System of record

CRM / LOS

Files arrive directly (portal upload, web form, or API) or through automated email routing.

View-only

Files cannot be downloaded. User permissions allow documents to be opened and reviewed inside the CRM or LOS only.

Downloadable

Files can be downloaded. Users can save documents from the CRM or LOS to a local device.

Requirement met

Files stay inside the system of record. No download safeguard needed.

Safeguards needed

A download safeguard must be in place, such as watermarking or browser and device controls.
