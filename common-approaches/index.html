Example Safeguards for Downloadable Documents
Transfer methods

Company-managed devices restrict common ways documents could be moved outside the company. Examples include:

  • Restricting access to logging into personal email accounts
  • Restricting access to personal cloud-storage accounts (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.).
  • Outbound email quarantine on messages with attachments sent to external and personal domains.
  • Removable-media restrictions — blocking copying to USB drives or other external storage.
Watermarking

Downloaded documents contain the company name, logo, or another identifying mark — Aquamark or equivalent. Awareness that documents can be traced back to the company creates a reasonable deterrent to misuse.

Activity monitoring + policy

User activity, including document downloads, is logged and subject to review, alerts, or other oversight. Personnel also sign a policy acknowledging that their activity is monitored. The principle is similar to visible security cameras: knowing your actions are being observed and can be traced back to you helps deter misconduct. Sample policy

Other

Every company operates differently. If you use another approach, contact us or describe the safeguard in the applicant portal for review.

Additional Enterprise Controls

Larger organizations, companies with multiple locations, or teams with more complex environments may already use broader enterprise security controls. These are additional options, not certification requirements.

  • CASB / Security Service Edge controls (e.g. Netskope) — applies access and data controls across cloud applications, such as restricting personal accounts, downloads, uploads, syncing, or use from unmanaged devices.
  • Enterprise browser or browser isolation platforms (e.g. Island) — can restrict downloads, copy/paste, screenshots, uploads, or other data movement within specific apps.
  • Data Loss Prevention (DLP) platforms (e.g. Microsoft Purview DLP) — detects sensitive information and applies rules to prevent or restrict unauthorized sharing through email, uploads, cloud apps, or other transfer methods.
  • MDM / endpoint management platforms (e.g. Intune, Jamf) — centrally applies and enforces device-level security policies regardless of the network being used.
  • Centrally managed browser policies (e.g. Chrome Enterprise/Google Admin, Microsoft Intune, Group Policy, Edge for Business) — restrict browser sign-in to approved company accounts, block specific websites, or enforce other browser-level controls.
  • Secure web gateways / DNS filtering — rules enforced through a firewall, secure web gateway, DNS filtering service, or managed device that block access to selected domains such as personal email or cloud-storage sites.

Employee Notice: System Activity Monitoring

As part of our commitment to protecting the confidentiality of applicant, customer, and financial information, [Company Name] monitors employee activity within our systems of record, including document access, downloads, and related actions.

This monitoring supports our obligations to safeguard sensitive information and helps us detect and prevent unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of confidential records.

By accessing these systems, you acknowledge that:

  • Your activity — including which documents you view, download, or otherwise interact with — may be logged and reviewed.
  • This monitoring is conducted for legitimate business and compliance purposes, not for evaluating unrelated job performance.
  • You are expected to access only the files and records necessary to perform your assigned role.

Questions about this notice may be directed to [designated contact].
