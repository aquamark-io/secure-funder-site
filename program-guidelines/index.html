Program Requirements

Secure Funder is not software and does not require specific software.

The basics

  • A program, not softwareNothing to install or integrate. We certify how your company handles sensitive files.
  • Evidence-basedSafeguards are verified through documents and screenshots uploaded to the member portal. PII and proprietary details can be redacted.
  • Your tools, your choiceWe don’t require specific tools. You decide how to meet each requirement.

The focus

The guidelines focus on the initial application package you receive from brokers—where misuse most often occurs.

The requirement

If the application package can be downloaded from an email inbox or your system of record, it should not be able to leave the company through:

  • Personal email
  • Cloud storage
  • USB drives

We look for this in your evidence, or work with you to put safeguards in place. If files are view-only, this requirement is already met.

Applies to non-management roles. Owners and upper management are not included.
