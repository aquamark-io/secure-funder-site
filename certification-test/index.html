Certification Quiz & Guidelines

Review the guidelines and test your status, now with vulnerability detection.

Program Scope
Guidelines are for deal-processing roles only (e.g. underwriters, sales, etc.).

The program focuses on the initial submission package, where unauthorized use most often occurs. If documents such as applications and bank statements are downloadable rather than view-only, we verify that reasonable safeguards are in place or help you identify an acceptable approach.

No specific method or software is required. The goal is to reduce the ability for personnel to download a file (email and system of record) and move it outside company systems, including through a browser using their personal email or cloud storage (e.g. Dropbox), or through external storage like USB.

How It Works
  • Submit request to join
  • Receive portal access
  • Listed in directory as an Applicant, newsletter and social announcements
  • Upload evidence within 90 days — extensions & support available
  • Get Certified — directory updated, digital badge issued, certification announced

Path to Certification

Join, then work toward certification with our support or self-service. The process is collaborative and focused on helping you meet the standard rather than finding reasons for disqualification.

Safeguarding the initial submission package.

Which safeguards are in place for downloadable submission documents?
Select any that apply, or choose Other to describe your approach.

Company-managed devices restrict common ways documents could be moved outside the company. Examples include:

  • Restricting access to logging into personal email accounts
  • Restricting access to personal cloud-storage accounts (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.).
  • Outbound email quarantine on messages with attachments sent to external and personal domains.
  • Removable-media restrictions — blocking copying to USB drives or other external storage.

Downloaded documents contain the company name, logo, or another identifying mark — Aquamark or equivalent. Awareness that documents can be traced back to the company creates a reasonable deterrent to misuse.

User activity, including document downloads, is logged and subject to review, alerts, or other oversight. Personnel also sign a policy acknowledging that their activity is monitored. The principle is similar to visible security cameras: knowing your actions are being observed and can be traced back to you helps deter misconduct. Sample policy

Every company operates differently. If you use another approach, tell us and we’ll provide feedback.

3. Personnel Controls

Basic personnel practices.
We’ll also ask if you have these. Companies are reviewed holistically, and a “No” response does not automatically prevent certification.

Background Checks

Background checks are part of your hiring process.

Screen Security

A policy or setting requiring screens to lock when unattended.

Off-Boarding Process

A process for promptly revoking access when personnel leave or change roles.

Final Review

A collaborative path to certification.

Please allow up to (5) business days for review. We’ll assess your evidence and confirm your business registration is active. If any evidence does not meet the intent of a safeguard, we’ll provide feedback, support, and ample time to address it.
