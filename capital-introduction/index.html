Member Benefit Overview

Access to Capital

Summary of the Secure Funder™ capital markets partnership

1.Credit Facilities

A credit facility is a structured financing arrangement in which an institutional capital provider extends a committed line of capital to a lender or specialty finance company. The facility is typically secured by the company's underlying loan portfolio or receivables and is designed to fund originations on a revolving or term basis.

Core components

  • Borrowing base — the pool of eligible assets that determines available capital
  • Advance rates — typically 70%–90% of eligible collateral
  • Cost of capital — SOFR plus spread, or a fixed rate depending on structure
  • Eligibility criteria — asset-level standards for inclusion in the borrowing base
  • Covenants & performance triggers — portfolio-level metrics monitored over the life of the facility
  • Facility structure — revolving, term, or senior-mezzanine configurations

Strategic benefits

  • Scalable origination volume without proportional equity dilution
  • Improved return on equity through institutional leverage
  • Consistent liquidity across market cycles
  • Greater balance sheet efficiency

2.Forward Flow Agreements

A forward flow agreement is a contractual arrangement in which a capital provider commits to purchase newly originated assets on an ongoing basis at predefined terms. Forward flow arrangements provide a predictable exit for originated deals and are often used in combination with a credit facility.

Core components

  • Purchase commitment — the volume of assets the buyer agrees to acquire over a defined period
  • Pricing structure — purchase price methodology established in advance
  • Eligibility criteria — asset-level requirements for qualifying deals
  • Settlement timing — typically 0–5 days from closing
  • Servicing structure — defines whether the originator retains servicing post-sale
  • True sale structure — assets transfer cleanly off the originator's balance sheet

Strategic benefits

  • Immediate liquidity on originated deals
  • Risk transfer to the purchasing counterparty
  • A predictable, contractual exit strategy
  • Scalable growth without accumulating portfolio risk

3.Integrated Capital Strategy

Credit facilities and forward flow agreements are often used together to create a complete originate-and-exit cycle. A credit facility provides the capital to fund new originations. A forward flow agreement provides a committed buyer for those originations. Proceeds from the sale pay down the facility, freeing capacity to originate again.

The cycle — originate, fund, sell, recycle, scale — is designed to compound origination capacity over time without requiring a proportional increase in equity.

4.Additional Capital Markets Services

Beyond credit facilities and forward flow, the partner firm also supports members with:

  • Debt and equity capital raises
  • Customized capital stack design for each stage of growth
  • Structuring advice on complex or non-standard transactions
  • Direct introductions to institutional capital relationships

5.Member Eligibility & Process

Access to this benefit is available to all Secure Funder™ members and applicants in good standing, at no additional cost. The introduction process is informal: members submit an inquiry, and Secure Funder connects them directly with the partner firm. From that point forward, all diligence, structuring, and terms are handled between the member and the partner.

Scope of the partnership. Secure Funder™ facilitates introductions only. We do not provide capital, broker transactions, participate in negotiations, or receive compensation based on any funding outcome. All terms, fees, and engagement structures are established directly between the member and the partner firm.

6.Requesting an Introduction

Members interested in learning more may submit a request through our standard contact channel. Please reference "Access to Capital" in your message and include a brief description of the type of financing or capital markets activity you would like to explore.

Submit a request at securefunder.org/contact.
