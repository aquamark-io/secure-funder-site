›Texas Registration · Disclosure Providers and brokers of sales-based financing Disclosure under $1,000,000 Registration deadline Dec. 31, 2026
Regulator
Office of Consumer Credit Commissioner (OCCC)
Who
Providers and brokers of sales-based financing
Deal size
Disclosure under $1,000,000
  • HB 700. Providers and brokers of commercial sales-based financing register with the OCCC through NMLS by December 31, 2026, and renew annually. Applications include a business plan and filings for key individuals (including 10%+ direct owners).
  • Providers give a written disclosure with specific offers under $1 million: total amount financed, finance charge, total repayment, fees, and repayment terms. Contracts must include a notice with OCCC contact information.
  • Automatic debits are prohibited unless the provider holds a perfected, first-priority security interest in the recipient’s accounts receivable. No de minimis exemption.

Deal size is the offer amount at or below which the law applies. Most states also exempt banks and very low-volume providers.

Good to Know

Registration vs. licensing

Registration is usually a filing with basic company and ownership information, renewed each year. Licensing adds review of financials, background, and conduct, with ongoing examination by the regulator.

States not listed

A state not listed here has no commercial-financing-specific statute today. General laws still apply, including unfair and deceptive practices laws, usury rules for loans, and existing broker or lender licensing for commercial loans in some states.

Where the deal is

These laws generally follow the location of the business receiving the financing, not where the provider or broker is based.

NMLS

Several states, including California, Connecticut, and Texas, use the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System. A single NMLS company record can support filings in multiple states.
