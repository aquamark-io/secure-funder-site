Funder certification guidelines

No specific software or tools required, including Aquamark, the programs Administrator. Guidelines public for transparency.

Join Program

Program applies only to deal-processing roles — sales, processing, underwriting, BPO teams.

Average Time to Complete
Guidelines already met: 1–2 hours
Guidelines not yet met: 1–2 days

How It Works
  • Submit a request to join the program.
  • Your company is added to the directory as an Applicant and featured in a program announcement.
  • Receive access to the member portal.
  • The portal asks a few questions about your process, then shows exactly what's needed for your situation.
  • Upload evidence within 3 months — screenshots, screen recordings, documents. Redacted PII acceptable.
  • Extensions and full support available.
  • Certification — your directory status is updated, you receive a digital badge, and your certification is formally announced through the programs newsletter and social media.

Path to Certification

Join, then work toward certification with our support. Each section includes examples, but alternative approaches are welcome if they meet the intent of the requirement. The process is collaborative and focused on helping you meet the standard rather than finding reasons for disqualification.

Requirement 01

Personnel Controls

Basic personnel practices.

Background Checks

Documentation showing that background checks are part of the hiring process.

Ways to demonstrate this
  • Blank copy of your background check authorization form.
  • Screenshot from your provider showing active account or recent checks, with PII redacted.

Password Policy

A password policy or system setting that requires strong passwords.

Ways to demonstrate this
  • Copy of password policy or screenshot of admin panel showing enforcement settings.

Workstation & Screen Security

A policy or technical setting requiring workstations to lock when unattended.

Ways to demonstrate this
  • Screenshot showing automatic screen locking enabled on company devices, or through an administrative system.
Recommended: Organizations are encouraged to maintain a policy prohibiting the use of cell phone cameras at workstations.

Off-Boarding Process

A process for promptly removing access when personnel leave the company or change roles.

Ways to demonstrate this
  • Offboarding checklist or policy.
Requirement 02

System Access

MFA on systems where deal documents are accessible.

Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA must be enabled on email, CRM, portal, or equivalent systems used by personnel who access merchant documents.

Ways to demonstrate this
  • Screenshot of MFA setting enabled in the relevant admin panel(s).
  • Screen recording of a login showing MFA prompt triggering and being completed.

Role-Based Access

Personnel should only be able to access accounts and information required for their role.

Ways to demonstrate this
  • Screenshot of roles or permission groups in your CRM.
  • Screen recording comparing two roles in the CRM.
Requirement 03

Document & Data Protection

Applies to personnel who process deals.
Scope: Covers documents and screenshots (e.g. PDF, DOC, JPG), and data exports (CSV, Excel) — wherever they mainly flow and are stored once a deal is submitted.

Safeguard

Depending on your process, a single safeguard, or combination, is sufficient.

Example safeguards
  • View-only access, no download option.
  • Secure delivery — documents are only shared through webforms, links, or API.
  • Controls on company-managed devices that deter external sharing — blocking personal email, personal cloud storage, or USB drives.
  • Outbound email settings that quarantine messages with attachments sent to external and personal domains.
  • Document watermarking (PDF only), using Aquamark's watermarking tools or equivalent.
  • Attachments route automatically to a CRM or equivalent, limiting manual access.
Program Notice

Final Review

A collaborative path to certification.

Please allow up to five business days for review. We’ll assess your evidence, confirm your business registration, and conduct limited public-record checks. If any evidence does not meet the intent of a safeguard, we’ll provide feedback and ample time to address it.
