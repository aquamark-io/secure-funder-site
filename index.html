FAQ

What is the value of certification?

Independent verification. Show customers and partners that your data protection safeguards have been independently verified. Certified companies receive a digital badge, a public directory listing, and a certification announcement on the program’s social media channels and in its newsletter.

How does certification help prevent backdooring?

We verify that companies have safeguards in place that make it harder for staff to take sensitive documents out of company systems and send them to themselves for resale.

What are the requirements?

Registered with your Secretary of State, have a website, and meet all of the program’s technical requirements. See the technical requirements here.

How does verification work?

Log in to the member portal, answer the questions, and upload your evidence. We’ll review what you submit and send you a report with our findings.

What if our evidence doesn’t meet the requirements?

No problem. We’ll explain what’s missing and give you time to make adjustments and resubmit. Our goal is to help you meet the standards and earn certification, not look for reasons to disqualify you.

Can we join before we meet the requirements?

Yes, we encourage it. You have 90 days to meet the requirements. You can ask for more time or book a free call for help. We’ll certify your company once we confirm you meet all of the requirements.

How much does it cost, and when is payment due?

$2,499 a year. You can pay monthly. Payment is due after you join, as the next step after Getting Started in your member portal.

Can staff still download documents?

Yes, as long as you have appropriate safeguards in place.

How does renewal work?

Certification renews every year. We’ll email you to ask if your systems or the way you store or protect sensitive data have changed. Tell us what changed so we can review it. If nothing has changed, confirm that and your payment will renew automatically.

Christina Duncan

Christina Duncan has worked in alternative business finance since 2009, including serving as COO of two working capital brokerages, one with an in-house funding division and more than 100 employees.

In 2025, she founded Aquamark to address data and deal theft in the industry. That work led to Secure Funder™ and Secure Broker™, independent certification programs focused on practical standards for protecting merchant information.

Today, Christina’s work focuses on advancing transparency and accountability across the industry, advocating for measures such as broker registration, and helping create an environment where small business owners can seek alternative capital with greater confidence and better outcomes.
