About

Why Secure Funder Exists

Alternative finance has grown rapidly, but unlike traditional banking, there is no regulatory body overseeing how organizations handle sensitive information. As a result, misuse of data has become a serious and persistent problem in the industry.

Program Director

With nearly 20 years in alternative finance, Christina brings deep operational experience to the Secure Funder program — from ISO operations and funder relationships to the day-to-day realities of how merchant data moves through the industry. She founded Aquamark to address the data security challenges she witnessed firsthand, and created Secure Funder to give the industry a practical framework for self-governance.
