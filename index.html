Our mission is to help establish a standard for protecting merchant data in alternative finance. Unlike traditional lending, the industry has lacked clear expectations around handling sensitive information, allowing backdooring and other forms of data misuse to persist. Secure Funder™ gives the market a clear way to identify responsible funders and makes it harder for bad actors to operate without accountability.

ABOUT

With nearly 20 years in alternative finance, Christina brings deep operational experience spanning ISO operations, funder relationships, and the day-to-day realities of how merchant data moves through the industry. She founded Aquamark to address industry-wide data security challenges she witnessed firsthand, and created Secure Funder and Secure Broker to establish a practical framework for self-governance.
