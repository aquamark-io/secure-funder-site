FAQ

Common questions about Secure Funder™.

What is Secure Funder™?

An independent certification that recognizes funders taking steps to prevent backdooring and data theft in alternative finance. It’s not software, and it doesn’t require any specific software.

What if I don’t meet the requirements yet?

Join now, and we’ll help. Most members have gaps to fill when they join, and closing them is the point of the program. You have 90 days to meet the requirements, and extensions and support are available.

Who can participate?

Companies in revenue-based financing, asset-based lending, equipment finance, factoring, and other non-bank capital.

What are the requirements?

Registration with your Secretary of State, a company website, and evidence of the technical requirements. The technical requirements apply only to people who handle deals. See the full requirements.

What are the technical requirements?

It depends on how your team accesses application files:

  • If files can be downloaded: they must not be able to leave the company through personal email, cloud storage, or USB drives. Use at least one of the acceptable safeguards: automated email intake, view-only access, watermarking, or browser and device controls. Other approaches are acceptable if they meet the same goal.
  • If files are view-only: no safeguards are needed. Just provide proof of your view-only settings.

We’ll also ask about multi-factor authentication for email and your system of record, screen lock policy, and how you remove access when someone leaves.

Is watermarking required?

No. Watermarking is not required to get certified. It’s one of several ways to protect downloadable files, alongside automated email intake, view-only access, and browser and device controls. If you do choose watermarking, any equivalent service works; you don’t have to use Aquamark.

How does verification work?

Two ways, at your own pace:

  • Self-service: answer the questions and upload your evidence in the member portal.
  • With our help: schedule a call with our support team, at no cost, to walk through your options and get guidance.

Either way, we independently review your safeguards and provide an assessment report.

What does it cost?

$2,499 USD per year. Monthly payments are available. There’s nothing to pay to join or to start verification; payment is due before the final review.

How does renewal work?

Certification renews annually. If your processes or tools have changed, you report the changes for review. If nothing has changed, you confirm that, and payment renews automatically.

Are refunds available?

No refunds are available once verification has begun, regardless of the outcome.

What do I get?

A digital badge, a directory listing, public announcements, an assessment report, member support, and industry and state compliance updates.

Can certification be revoked?

Yes, if required safeguards are no longer maintained or information provided during verification is found to be inaccurate. If certification is revoked or lapses, the badge and any mention of certified status must be removed.

Christina Duncan

Christina Duncan has worked in alternative business finance since 2009, including serving as COO of two working capital brokerages, one with an in-house funding division and more than 100 employees.

In 2025, she founded Aquamark to address data and deal theft in the industry. That work led to Secure Funder™ and Secure Broker™, independent certification programs focused on practical standards for protecting merchant information.

Today, Christina’s work focuses on advancing transparency and accountability across the industry, advocating for measures such as broker registration, and helping create an environment where small business owners can seek alternative capital with greater confidence and better outcomes.
