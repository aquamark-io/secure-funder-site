Our Mission

Secure Funder™ is a new industry initiative helping make responsible data handling visible in alternative finance. In a market with limited barriers and inconsistent oversight, data misuse continues to harm business owners and undermine funders who choose to operate with care. Paired with Secure Broker™, the programs independently verify companies taking practical steps to protect merchant data and operate responsibly.

The program certifies responsible data handling, not underwriting practices or broader business operations. Standards will evolve through a transparent, collaborative review process as new industry priorities emerge.
